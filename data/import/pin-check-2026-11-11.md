## Third pass, 2026-11-11 evening

Of the 48 circuits above:
- **12 moved by Wikidata + any mapped track at its point.** Many circuits are mapped only under a Japanese, Chinese or Korean name, which the name search can't match. The new check instead looks for a mapped raceway, track or sports centre within 1.5 km of Wikidata's point. Moved this way: Tsukuba, Sodegaura, Shanghai Tianma, Zhejiang (city now Keqiao), Nihonkai Maze, Ordos, Taebaek, Midvaal, Campo Grande (Orlando Moura), San Nicolás (Juan María Traverso), San Juan Villicum and Dos Cristais.

  - Hunan International Circuit was about 100 km off. It was renamed from Zhuzhou International Circuit in November 2025.
  - Franciacorta was 44 km off, in Lavenone; it is now in Castrezzato.
  - Autódromo Juan Manuel Fangio was in Rafaela; it is Rosario's municipal autodrome.
  - Rosendo Hernández was 70 km off; it is now in San Luis.
  - Also moved: Wanneroo, Velopark, Sportsland Yamanashi, Autoland Tsukude, Mihama, Tsukuruma Nasu, Circuit LFG and Velo Città.
- **2 are fine as they are:** Mount Panorama and DriveIt NQ, whose pins are within about 1 km of Wikidata's point.
